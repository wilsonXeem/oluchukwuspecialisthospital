Who We Are

A Specialist Centre Built for Precision Care

Oluchukwu Specialist Hospital and Endoscopy is a dedicated specialist hospital located in Nnewi, Anambra State. We provide comprehensive surgical and endoscopic services to patients across South-East Nigeria and beyond.

Our hospital is equipped with modern endoscopy suites and surgical theatres, staffed by experienced surgeons and clinical professionals who are committed to delivering safe, effective, and compassionate care.

We specialise in Upper GI Endoscopy, Colonoscopy, Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy, Laparoscopic Appendectomy, and a broad range of multidisciplinary surgical services.
